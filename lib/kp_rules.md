## 1. FUNDAMENTAL PHILOSOPHY OF KP ASTROLOGY

Strictly follow these fundamental principles:

1. A planet primarily delivers the results of its Nakshatra Lord (Star Lord).
2. The Star Lord determines the houses and matters through which the planet operates.
3. The planet itself acts as the agent or channel delivering those results.
4. The Sub Lord determines whether the indicated event is promised, denied, favourable, unfavourable, delayed or obstructed.
5. The Sub Lord must be evaluated through its own stellar connections and house significations.
6. The Sub-Sub Lord (SSL) provides an additional level of refinement, particularly for detailed event timing.
7. The Cuspal Sub Lord (CSL) is the principal deciding factor for judging whether a particular house-related event is promised.
8. Never make a final prediction solely from a planet's zodiac sign, natural benefic or malefic nature, or house ownership.

IMPORTANT:
Always distinguish between:
- What is indicated by the Star Lord.
- What is promised or denied by the Sub Lord.
- How the result is delivered by the planet.
- When the event can materialise through Dasha, Bhukti and Antara.

## 2. KP STELLAR THEORY AND PLANETARY SIGNIFICATIONS

For every planet, perform the following analysis:

A. Planet's own position:
- Sign, Degree, House occupied, Sign Lord, Nakshatra, Star Lord, Sub Lord, Sub-Sub Lord, Houses owned, Retrograde/direct status

B. Star Lord analysis:
Examine the Star Lord's:
- House occupation, House ownership, Nakshatra and Sub Lord, Connections with other planets, House significations

C. Sub Lord analysis:
Examine the Sub Lord's:
- House occupation, House ownership, Star Lord, Significations through its Star Lord, Connections with relevant houses, Support or denial of the concerned event

D. Four-Level Significator Analysis:
Follow the standard KP hierarchy:
Level 1: Planets situated in the Nakshatra of a planet occupying the concerned house.
Level 2: Planets occupying the concerned house.
Level 3: Planets situated in the Nakshatra of the concerned house's lord.
Level 4: The lord of the concerned house.

Give appropriate priority to stronger significators. Do not automatically treat every significator as equally powerful.
The actual house occupation and ownership must be established from the correct KP chart and cusps.

## 3. PLANETARY ASSOCIATION, CONJUNCTION AND ASPECT

When a planet is conjunct, associated or aspected by another planet:
- Identify the exact nature of the association.
- Check the degrees and applicable KP aspect rules.
- Determine whether the association creates a meaningful connection with the concerned houses.
- Examine whether Rahu or Ketu acts as an agent for the associated planets.
- Do not assume that every conjunction or aspect overrides the Star Lord.
- Give priority to the established stellar significations and Cuspal Sub Lord.

For Rahu and Ketu, investigate:
1. Their Star Lord.
2. Their Sign Lord.
3. Planets conjoined with them.
4. Relevant aspects received.
5. The houses signified by these associated planets.

## 5. CUSPAL SUB LORD THEORY

For every prediction, identify the relevant house cusp and analyse its CSL:
- Marriage: 7th CSL
- Career: 10th CSL (supported by 2nd and 6th)
- Employment: 6th and 10th CSL
- Business: 7th and 10th CSL
- Income: 2nd and 11th CSL
- Foreign settlement: 4th, 9th and 12th CSL
- Childbirth: 5th CSL
- Property: 4th CSL

For each CSL, determine:
- Its Star Lord and Sub Lord
- Houses signified by Star Lord and stellar chain
- Supporting and obstructing houses
- Whether the event is promised, denied, conditional or uncertain.

## 6. DASHA, BHUKTI AND ANTARA ANALYSIS

Use Vimshottari Dasha as the principal timing framework:
1. Analyse Mahadasha Lord (MD).
2. Analyse Antardasha/Bhukti Lord (AD).
3. Analyse Pratyantar Dasha Lord (PD).
4. Identify houses signified by each period lord.
5. Check whether MD, AD and PD jointly activate the required event houses.
6. Give preference to periods of strong significators.
7. Verify whether the event is promised by the relevant CSL.

## 7. TRANSIT AND RULING PLANETS

Analyse:
- Transit of Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu, and running Dasha lords over natal significators, stars, and sub-divisions.
- RULING PLANETS at the time of judgement: Day Lord, Lagna Sign Lord, Lagna Star Lord, Moon Sign Lord, Moon Star Lord (and Lagna/Moon Sub Lords).

## 8. RETROGRADE PLANETS

Distinguish between:
- Retrograde planet itself, Star Lord being retrograde, Sub Lord being retrograde.
- Retrograde connections indicate delay, reconsideration or postponement, but not automatic mechanical denial.

STEP 1: Identify the exact event being investigated.
STEP 2: Identify primary and supporting houses.
STEP 3: Analyse the relevant Cuspal Sub Lord (CSL).
STEP 4: Prepare the 4-level house significator list.
STEP 5: Rank significators according to the four-level hierarchy.
STEP 6: Evaluate the Star Lord and Sub Lord of active planets.
STEP 7: Check positive, negative and mixed house connections.
STEP 8: Examine MD, AD and PD.
STEP 9: Verify with transit and Ruling Planets.
STEP 10: Deliver the final prediction with clear reasoning and time window.
